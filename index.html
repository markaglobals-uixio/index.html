Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

Your privacy matters to us. Palette Studio is committed to protecting your personal information and being transparent about what data is — and is not — collected.

In short: Palette Studio runs entirely inside your browser. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any of your data. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking.

What We Collect

Nothing. Palette Studio collects no personal information, no browsing history, no page content, no device identifiers, and no usage statistics.

Your saved palettes and likes are stored only in your browser's local storage on your device and never leave it.

What We Don't Collect

  • No personal information (name, email, location).
  • No browsing history or visited website content.
  • No analytics, tracking cookies, or advertising identifiers.
  • No keystrokes, screenshots, or clipboard data.

Why We Collect Nothing

Palette Studio is designed to be a fully offline, local tool. Everything — palette generation, image-to-palette extraction, contrast checking, gradients, and color picking — is computed inside your browser using your device's processing power. The extension makes no network requests.

Where Your Data Lives

The only data involved is what you choose to create and save (for example, liked palettes). This data stays in your browser's local storage on your device. Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the extension removes it permanently.

Third-Party Services

None. Palette Studio does not use any third-party services, APIs, fonts, or analytics providers. Nothing is ever sent to an external server.

Children

Palette Studio is a general-purpose design tool and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13. Since we collect nothing at all, there is no data of a child's that we could hold.

Your Rights

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing to request, correct, or delete on our side. To remove everything, simply clear your browser data or uninstall the extension.
