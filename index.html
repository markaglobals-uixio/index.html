Privacy Policy

Convert Webpage to Figma — Chrome Extension & Figma Plugin • Last updated: 16 September 2026

Your privacy matters to us. Convert Webpage to Figma is committed to protecting your personal information and maintaining transparency about how data is collected and used.

What We Collect

  • Google account details — when you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address. These are used to identify your account and are stored locally in the extension on your device.
  • Device and IP address — a device identifier and your public IP address are detected and sent to our license server together with your account email. This is used to run the free trial and manage access (see "Why we collect it").
  • License records — first use date, last use date, plan status, login activity, and a count of how many times the extension was used to perform a capture.
  • Support messages — if you contact us, your message is sent through the channel you choose (WhatsApp or Slack) and we receive it so we can follow up.

We do not collect browsing history, page content, analytics, passwords, or payment card details.

Why We Collect It

  • To securely sign you in and identify your account.
  • To operate the free trial per device and per internet connection, and to unlock lifetime access after activation.
  • To prevent abuse, block unauthorized usage, and provide support.

Payments are arranged directly by email and processed outside the extension — we never see or store card or banking details.

Where Your Data Lives

Sign-in details stay in your browser's extension storage on your device. License and access records (email, IP, device identifier, dates, plan status, capture count) are stored in a private, developer-controlled Cloudflare Worker database, transmitted over HTTPS. Only the developer can access it.

Third-Party Services

To convert a webpage into a Figma design, the extension processes the page's HTML, CSS, fonts, and images locally in your browser. Certain resources may be retrieved through third-party services:

  • Google (accounts.google.com) — used for sign-in. Google shares your name and email address with us when you authorize it.
  • CORS proxy services (corsproxy.io, api.allorigins.win) — used to fetch remote images and assets that websites do not allow to be loaded directly. The requested image/asset URL is transmitted to these services.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) — used to load web fonts used on the captured page.
  • YouTube (img.youtube.com) — used to load video thumbnails for embedded videos.
  • WhatsApp (wa.me) — used only when you choose to contact us.
  • Slack (hooks.slack.com) — used to receive support and access-request messages you choose to send us so we can follow up.

These services only receive the data necessary to complete your request (e.g., the asset URL). We never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing.

Conversion Stays Local

All conversion work — reading the page's HTML, CSS, fonts, and images, and building the Figma design — happens entirely inside your browser. The page content itself is converted locally on your device and is never uploaded to our servers.

Google User Data — Limited Use

Convert Webpage to Figma's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention & Your Rights

License records are kept for as long as they are needed to operate your trial or lifetime access. You can request a copy of your data or ask for it to be deleted at any time by emailing us; deletion requests are honoured within 7 days. Signing out removes your Google details from the extension on your device.

Children

Convert Webpage to Figma is a professional design tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
